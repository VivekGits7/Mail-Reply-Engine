# Mail Reply Engine — Simple Screen-wise Script

## Starting me bolna

> "Ye HR team ke liye Mail Reply Engine hai. Isme HR ke daily emails aur kaam ek jagah manage hote hain. System email ka draft aur follow-up bana deta hai, lekin final decision HR ka hota hai."

---

## Left sidebar samjhana

> "Left side me saare sections hain. Desk me daily HR ka kaam hai, Programs me regular activities hain, Monitoring me system ka status hai, aur Admin me settings hain."

---

# 1. Desk

## Overview

> "Ye home screen hai. Yahan HR ko aaj ka important kaam dikhta hai."

> "Isme pending approvals, ready joiners, ready exits aur failed work ka count dikhta hai."

> "Matlab HR ko sabse pehle kya dekhna hai, wo yahan se pata chal jata hai."

## Approvals

> "Yahan system ke banaye hue email drafts aate hain."

> "HR email ko dekh kar approve ya reject karta hai. Approve ke baad hi email send hota hai."

> "Isse galat email automatically send nahi hota."

## Joiners

> "Ye new employee ke onboarding ka section hai."

> "Yahan naam, joining date, documents aur current status dikhta hai."

> "Agar kisi ka document pending hai, to HR yahan se dekh sakta hai."

> "Admin yahan se naya joiner add bhi kar sakta hai."

## Joiner detail / Case detail

> "Kisi joiner par click karenge to uski full detail khul jayegi."

> "Yahan uske documents, emails, files, pending tasks aur saari activity history dikhti hai."

> "Matlab ek person ka saara kaam ek hi place par mil jata hai."

## Exits

> "Ye employee exit ka section hai."

> "Jab koi employee leave karta hai, to IT, Finance aur doosri teams se clearance lena hota hai."

> "Yahan dekh sakte hain kis employee ka kaunsa clearance pending hai."

> "Sab clear hone ke baad employee exit complete kar sakte hain."

---

# 2. Programs

## Reminders

> "Yahan automatic reminder set karte hain."

> "Jaise timesheet bharna, training complete karna, ya appraisal ka reminder bhejna."

> "System selected people ko time par reminder bhej deta hai."

## Feedback

> "Yahan new joiners ka feedback manage hota hai."

> "30, 60 aur 90 days ke baad feedback liya ja sakta hai."

> "Agar feedback score low ho, to HR ko jaldi pata chal jata hai."

## Complaints

> "Yahan employee complaints aati hain."

> "Ye sensitive section hai, isliye sirf allowed HR users hi dekh sakte hain."

> "Yahan complaint ka status aur related details track karte hain."

---

# 3. Monitoring

## Activity Log

> "Yahan system ne kya-kya kaam kiya, uska record hota hai."

> "Jaise email kab gaya, kisne approval diya, ya kya update hua."

## Failures

> "Agar koi email send nahi hua ya system ka koi kaam fail hua, wo yahan dikhega."

> "Problem solve hone ke baad yahin se uska kaam dobara chala sakte hain."

---

# 4. Admin

## Controls

> "Ye safety screen hai."

> "Kill Switch se saare outgoing emails ek saath stop kar sakte hain."

> "Dry Run me real person ko email bhejne ke bajay test inbox me email jaati hai."

## Settings

> "Yahan system ke basic rules set karte hain."

> "Jaise follow-up kab bhejna hai, email kis time bhejni hai, aur team details kya hain."

## Email Templates

> "Yahan ready-made email messages hain."

> "HR yahan email ka text change kar sakta hai, jaise document request ya reminder mail."

## Checklists

> "Yahan onboarding ke liye required documents ki list hoti hai."

> "Jaise ID proof, education documents, ya baaki joining documents."

## Induction Plans

> "Yahan new joiner ke first week ke sessions plan hote hain."

> "Jaise HR introduction, IT setup aur team introduction."

## Holidays

> "Yahan company holidays add karte hain."

> "Taaki system holiday wale din email ya reminder na bheje."

## Users

> "Yahan users aur unka access manage hota hai."

> "Admin decide karta hai kaun kya dekh sakta hai."

---

## End me bolna

> "Simple words me, ye system HR ka daily email aur follow-up work easy banata hai. HR ko saare joiners, exits, reminders aur pending emails ek jagah milte hain. Final approval aur control hamesha HR ke paas rehta hai."

---

1. Overview
2. Approvals
3. Joiners → kisi ek person ki detail
4. Exits
